# Author Review And Signal Quality Control

The inherited methods and numerical analyses were reviewed by human authors. This release does not represent two independent physician adjudications or a clinician-signed reference standard. Startup screening and masking are rule-based signal-quality sensitivities. These checks do not establish the clinical cause of low-amplitude recordings.

The current creator list and Tao Xu acknowledgement follow the author update requested for this release. Publication status and the version DOI are verified externally in the release record.
