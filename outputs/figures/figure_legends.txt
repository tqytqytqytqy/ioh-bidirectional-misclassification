Figure 1. Derivation of the waveform cohort and recorded infusion-adjustment subset. Exclusions in the left panel are sequential and mutually exclusive. The right panel shows cases with target-pump records, observed rate changes, adjustment blocks meeting quality criteria, low-reference events and confirmed recoveries. The number of cases with pump records is distinct from the number of eligible adjustment events. MAP, mean arterial pressure.

Figure 2. Event visibility and aggregate exposure across sampling intervals. Panel a shows the phase-averaged percentage of reference episodes with any low display, including inherited low values. Panel b shows displayed duration and deficit area as percentages of their respective continuous-reference totals. The panels report different quantities and do not share a clinical scale. Reference episodes lasted at least 60 s below 65 mmHg; duration and area include all low-pressure bins, including shorter runs. Unavailable initial display contributes no displayed burden. Uncertainty estimates and unrounded values are reported in Supplemental Digital Content 2, workbook sheets Legacy_S26 and Interval_Unrounded. AUC, area under the deficit curve.

Figure 3. New low samples, inherited display and variation across cases with a 5 min display. Panel a assigns each phase-episode pair to one mutually exclusive source category; a new low sample takes priority when both sources occur. Panel b shows the distribution of phase-averaged miss proportions for individual cases. Panel c shows each case's maximum-minus-minimum range across sampling phases, in percentage points. In panels b and c, the thick segment denotes the interquartile range and the point the median. Thin whiskers in panel b denote the 5th and 95th percentiles, not confidence limits. All qualifying episodes lasted at least 60 s below 65 mmHg; the case distributions include 1899 cases. Cases with few events can have wide phase ranges.

Figure 4. Display states at infusion adjustments with low reference pressure and after recovery. Low reference denotes mean arterial pressure below 65 mmHg. Panel a shows four mutually exclusive phase-averaged states at 236 low-reference adjustments from 83 subjects. The 0.2% unavailable fraction at 5 min is included but is too small to label within the bar. A new low value was acquired during the current contiguous low run before adjustment; an inherited value was acquired before that run. Panel b shows mean restricted low-display persistence in 184 recovery events from 78 subjects; bars are 95% subject-cluster bootstrap confidence intervals. Recovery requires two consecutive normal completed bins. Observation ends at normal display, missing reference, recurrent hypotension or 5 min after adjustment. The plots describe emulated display information at recorded adjustment and recovery time points.
